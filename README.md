*A study of the electric scooter's impact on road traffic safety at an uncontrolled intersection*

## At a glance

- **Question:** Do e-scooters make urban traffic less safe for vulnerable road users?
- **Site:** An uncontrolled intersection in Malmö, Sweden.
- **Methods:**
  - Video analysis in T-Analyst of 100 e-scooter riders, with 100 cyclists as a comparison group (speed, behaviour, interactions)
  - A field survey and a panel survey, analysed in SPSS, including Chi-square tests for gender differences
- **Findings:**
  - E-scooters rode faster than walking pace on sidewalks.
  - More men than women exceeded Sweden's 20 km/h legal limit.
  - Speeds were similar for e-scooter riders and cyclists.
  - Dangerous interactions were rare, which suggests e-scooters did not pose a major danger at this site.
  - The survey showed gaps in public knowledge about e-scooters in Malmö and Lund.
